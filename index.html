1U CubeSat engineering model, ground-based prototype, not flown. Orbit, gamma and flare inputs, and the LoRa link are simulated; nothing is transmitted.
Alert rules and sources

Alert rules: UV WARNING at 8 or above. Field change WARNING above 5 µT, ALERT above 10 µT (5-point average, placeholder baseline 50 µT). Gamma threat WARNING above 26.5 CPM, ALERT above 30.7 CPM. Flare index WARNING at 50, ALERT at 80. Overall level is the highest channel level.

Sources: payload values are labelled SIM unless the incoming packet says "source": "LIVE". Gamma and flare are simulated threat inputs, not payloads. Units: UV index (WHO), µT, CPM, °C. Times UAE (UTC+4). Not reference-grade.

Paste JSON readings

One packet, an array of packets, or one packet per line. Keys: uv_index, field_delta_uT, temp_C (optional gamma_cpm, flare_index, source, seq). Packets without "source": "LIVE" are shown as SIM.
